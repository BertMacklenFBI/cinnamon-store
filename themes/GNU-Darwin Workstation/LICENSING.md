The shipped wallpaper is a procedural solid black image. The menu image is a lossless conversion of GNUstepGlow.xpm by Banlu Kemiyatorn (2000), under WTFPL-1. ARTWORK-WindowMaker-COPYRIGHT preserves the full upstream notice; ARTWORK-assets-report.json binds the shipped menu image to its source hash and credit.

The public provenance review passed for this exact desktop theme, wallpaper and menu artwork scope on 2026-09-30. Icons, cursor binaries, font binaries, Fastfetch artwork and boot/login artwork are outside the package. Component artwork terms remain separate from the theme source license.
